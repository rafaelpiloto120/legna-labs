Where it started

One printer.
One useful idea at a time.

3DPiloto began in 2021 with a single 3D printer and the curiosity to make a few things. When those first prints found an audience, the experiments became an Etsy shop.

That same process still guides the project: notice a small everyday problem, model a practical answer, print it and refine the details. The result is a growing collection for tidier desks, better displays and hobbies worth making room for.
