A few things to know

Before you make it yours.

How fresh are the prices?

TCG Ticker checks for updated market data hourly. The source data itself may update less frequently, so prices can remain unchanged between checks.

Will the price match my exact card or listing?

Displayed prices are market estimates and may differ from individual listings or completed sales because of condition, language, seller location, shipping and market changes. Fallback pricing may be used when an exact price is unavailable.

How many cards can I follow?

Save up to ten cards in the online customer portal and display up to five cards in rotation on your TCG Ticker.

What do I need to set it up?

A USB power source, a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and access to the online customer portal. A USB-C cable, printed setup guide and device code card are included.
